I am Professor of Media & Communication at Muhlenberg College in Allentown, PA. My research explores the links between media industries and systems of social and economic power. Specifically, I leverage critical media industry studies to explore the impacts of professionalization, specialization, and platformization on online digital creator cultures, particularly in the podcasting industry. I recently published a book entitled [Podcasting in a Platform Age](https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/podcasting-in-a-platform-age-9781501380679/), published by Bloomsbury Academic in 2024. The book examines podcasting production in the United States, focusing on the processes of formalization and monetization of amateur and semi-professional (or “Pro-Am”) labor. Other publications include [Media Audiences, 2nd edition](https://www.sagepub.com/shop/buy-a-book/media-audiences-2-258868) (Sage, 2019), an undergraduate text exploring the theoretical landscape of audience studies, and [The Platforms of Podcasting: Past and Present](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/2056305119880002) (*Social Media + Society*, 2019).
