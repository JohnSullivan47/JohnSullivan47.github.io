I am Professor of Media & Communication at Muhlenberg College in Allentown, PA. My research explores the links between media industries and systems of social and economic power. Specifically, I leverage critical media industry studies to explore the impacts of professionalization, specialization, and platformization on online digital creator cultures, particularly in the podcasting industry. I recently published a book entitled Podcasting in a Platform Age, published by Bloomsbury Academic in 2024. The book examines podcasting production in the United States, focusing on the processes of formalization and monetization of amateur and semi-professional (or “Pro-Am”) labor. Other publications include Media Audiences, 2nd edition (Sage, 2019), an undergraduate text exploring the theoretical landscape of audience studies, and The Platforms of Podcasting: Past and Present (Social Media + Society, 2019).
